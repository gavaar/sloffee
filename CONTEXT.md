# Game

A Tamagotchi-like game intended for short daily visits, with mini-games contributing to progression. Specific progression rewards and persistence mechanics are not yet defined.

## Language

**Daily session**:
A short visit in which a player plays and makes progress, intended to last around 15 minutes before they leave and return another day. This is a design goal, not a defined daily time or attempt limit.

**Mini-game**:
A constrained challenge with a beat-or-fail outcome. The nature of its constraints is not yet defined.

**Meta-progression**:
Progress earned across mini-games: participation earns some, and beating a mini-game earns more. Its form, such as whether it is an in-game currency, is not yet defined.
